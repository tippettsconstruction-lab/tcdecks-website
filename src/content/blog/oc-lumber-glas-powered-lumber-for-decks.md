For most of deck building history, the choice for framing was simple: wood or, more recently, steel. OC Lumber from Owens Corning adds a third option. TC Decks is a member of the OC Pro Build Team, so we get to work with it directly. Here's what it is and where it fits.

## What OC Lumber is

OC Lumber is a building material made by Owens Corning, the company best known for fiberglass. They call it **Glas-Powered Lumber**. It's a composite building material made with glass fiber instead of wood, designed to take the place of wood in outdoor building.

## Wood-free, rust-free, rot-proof

Those three words sum up why it's interesting for decks:

- **Wood-free.** There's no wood in it, so there's nothing to rot, split or attract termites.
- **Rust-free.** Unlike steel, there's no metal to corrode, even where water and fasteners meet.
- **Rot-proof.** Water and moisture don't break it down. That matters most in the places wood frames fail first: where joists meet boards, ledgers meet the house, and posts meet the ground.

## How it compares to wood and steel

**Versus pressure-treated wood.** Wood is cheap and easy to work, but it rots, warps and shrinks over time. OC Lumber is built to avoid all of that.

**Versus steel framing.** We're big fans of [steel framing](/blog/steel-deck-framing-fortress-evolution) too. OC Lumber is built for a similar long life, with no metal to corrode.

The right choice depends on the deck, the design and the site. We'll walk you through the options when we talk about your project.
